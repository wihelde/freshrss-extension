# Product

## Register

product

## Users

FreshRSS users reading foreign-language articles in the existing FreshRSS interface.

## Product Purpose

Translate one open article on demand without leaving FreshRSS, reloading the page, or persisting article content.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, familiar, functional. The extension inherits FreshRSS instead of introducing its own visual identity.

## Anti-references

Floating controls, prominent decoration, and interactions that compete with FreshRSS article actions.

## Design Principles

- Put article actions alongside existing article actions.
- Preserve the reader's place and original content.
- Reuse FreshRSS interaction and visual conventions.
- Keep status and failures close to the affected article.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native buttons, visible disabled states, readable status text, and keyboard-accessible interactions.
